AI Story-to-Video Workflow 🎬

An experimental project exploring how AI can make animated storytelling and short-film production more accessible to independent creators.

About the Project

I've always been interested in storytelling and animation, but creating animated videos traditionally requires a lot of time, technical skills, and resources.

I started this project to experiment with AI tools and find a simpler way to turn an idea or written story into an engaging animated video.

The goal is to bring different parts of the creative process together, from writing the story to generating scenes and producing the final video.

What I'm Exploring

- Story Development: Creating original stories with engaging plots, characters, and endings.
- Character Consistency: Developing prompts and references to maintain consistent characters across scenes.
- Scene Generation: Experimenting with AI video generation tools.
- Voiceovers: Creating narration and character dialogue.
- Sound Design: Using sound effects and background music to improve storytelling.
- Video Production: Organizing individual scenes into complete short videos.

Why This Project?

I believe creative tools should be accessible to everyone, not just people with expensive equipment or professional production teams.

AI has made it possible for individuals to experiment with ideas that were previously difficult or expensive to execute.

I want to explore these possibilities and document what works, what doesn't, and how the process can be improved.

Current Status

This project is in its early experimental stage. I'm continuously testing different approaches, improving prompts, and documenting the workflow as I learn.

Future Goals

- Improve consistency between generated scenes.
- Experiment with better animation and synchronization.
- Simplify the overall production process.
- Explore more efficient AI-assisted storytelling workflows.
- Share useful findings with other independent creators.

Contributions

Suggestions, feedback, and ideas are welcome. If you're also experimenting with AI storytelling or video generation, feel free to share your thoughts.
